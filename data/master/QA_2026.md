## Reconciliations and safeguards

- England: retrieved Championship, League One and League Two first (24 clubs each); independently revisited the Premier League source (20). Dynamic official EFL tables did not expose usable rosters, so season-specific secondary tables were used, not guessed promotions.
- France: the Ligue 2 preseason article exposed only 16 clubs; the separate official 18-club article supplied Annecy and Red Star. Count is 18, not 16.
- Czechia: an early fixture headline conflicted with current placement of Artis/Karvina. The current [official CHNL table](https://www.chnliga.cz/tabulka/2026?id_type=1&order_direction=1) agrees with all 16 secondary tier-2 entries, including Karvina and excluding Artis. Secondary status is retained because the retrieved official page did not expose its selected season label.
- Belgium: 18 top-tier and 15 second-tier clubs are sourced; older 16-team assumptions were not reused. Wales has 16 and Gibraltar 11 in the retrieved current-season tables.
- GIB-BRU is a reserved duplicate alias of GIB-FCB; no second membership is created. [UEFA uses FCB Magpies](https://es.uefa.com/nationalassociations/teams/2606279--magpies/); the current federation table uses FC Magpies. [The alternate Bruno's Magpies name](https://www.zerozero.pt/equipa/bruno-s-magpies/93073) was checked. Historical IDs/names are retained with an explicit redirect.
- Vaduz retains LIE-VAD for Swiss league membership; FC Andorra receives an AND- code for Spanish membership. Inherited ENG-/FRA-/IRL-/WAL- namespaces are preserved for legacy cross-border cases, not represented as geographical assertions; see REGISTRY_SCOPE.md.
- AFC Wimbledon is not merged with historical Wimbledon FC. First teams and reserve sides (Jong/B/II/[A]) remain separate entities. Such reserve sides are admitted members of the researched senior leagues, not separate youth-league scope.
- Names from source tables are retained separately from canonical names. Source abbreviations are not expanded speculatively. Kharkiv and FK Metalist are distinct source entries and IDs; [the Kharkiv fan-community page](https://en.fckharkiv1925.com/) describes the former Metalist 1925 name, not the separate Metalist identity.
- Historical seed rows, numeric legacy aliases, out-of-scope competitions and provider mappings were preserved. Absence from this snapshot means outside current scoped rosters, not necessarily an inactive club.

## Limitations / remaining evidence-quality risk

66 divisions rely on a retrieved secondary season table. Full scope completion is therefore **not 87 officially certified rosters**. A consumer requiring only federation-confirmed membership must filter by VERIFIED_OFFICIAL; upgrading the other 66 is a separate evidence-strengthening step. Source pages can change, and crawled tables may lag; this deliverable freezes names/URLs/retrieval dates, not standings or future membership. No independent second-source check is claimed for every league. Technical QA cannot establish the truth of a source by itself.

No cup draw memberships, odds, provider mappings, scanner integration or dashboard integration are included. These are outside the requested domestic-division data task. Russia's domestic listing does not override UEFA eligibility restrictions.
